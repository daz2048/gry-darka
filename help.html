Minecraft 0.2 / 0.3 and Klocki Podlasia 0.1

Explore and build with unlimited materials. This is creative mode, with no survival, crafting, or multiplayer. Play on a computer with a keyboard, mouse, and WebGL2. Minecraft 0.3 targets Chrome on macOS, not phones.

  • WASD / arrows move; Space jumps; move the mouse to look around.
  • Left click / Q removes a block; right click / E places one. 1–8 selects a material; the wheel cycles materials. In 0.2 / 0.3, you can also choose palette sets.
  • Choose “Play — capture mouse.” If mouse capture does not work, choose “Play without capturing,” drag to look around, and use Q / E to edit.
  • Esc / P pauses and releases the mouse. H / “Games menu” leaves the game. “Back to start” can help if you get stuck.

Saves: Versions 0.1 and 0.2 do not save builds. In 0.3, check the save status, choose worlds from the list, and use “Export” to download an external backup. “Import world” creates a separate copy instead of overwriting an existing one. Browser data is local to the site address, device, and profile; localhost and the online site do not share saves. Export before clearing browser data.

Flappy Podlasie 0.1 / Flappy Bird 0.2

Fly through gates or pipes without hitting them. Press the start button, then click or tap the playfield, or press Space / ↑ to flap. Short, steady inputs help you maintain altitude.

  • P / the pause button pauses and resumes. H / Esc leaves for the games menu.
  • 0.1: M toggles sound.
  • 0.2: S toggles sound and music; B opens the shop; M toggles turbo and invincibility. Skins are cosmetic.

High scores and settings are stored in this browser. In 0.2, coins and purchased skins are local too; there are no accounts or cloud sync.

Mario 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3 — Maks Jump Quest

Collect coins, avoid enemies, and reach the exit gate. Use arrows or A / D to move; Space, W, or ↑ to jump. Touch devices have movement and jump buttons.

P pauses; R starts a new run; H / Esc returns to the games menu. In 0.2 / 0.3, S opens the shop, and Esc closes it first. Version 0.1 has three worlds; 0.2 / 0.3 have 100 levels each.

Archers 0.1–0.4 — Ragdoll Shooters

Aim with the mouse, hold to charge, and release to fire. Your rival shoots on its own timer and does not wait for you. Defeat rivals and spend points on arrows; versions 0.2–0.4 also have defense upgrades.

Use ❚❚ / ▶ to pause or resume a duel. “Restart” starts a new run. Use the top-bar back arrow to return to the games menu.

0.3 / 0.4: N grants maximum armor and invincibility until restart. 0.4: equip one armor type at a time; Barrier lasts 5 seconds, Cleanse removes harmful effects, and Full Repair restores health. Read shop descriptions for each effect.

Zombie Onslaught, Zombie Max, and Zombie Aleksa

Defend your base against waves of zombies. Move the squad vertically with ↑ / ↓, the mouse wheel, or by dragging on the playfield. The squad fires automatically. The Aleksa variant also supports W / S. Use upgrades to strengthen your team.

  • P pauses/resumes; R restarts; F toggles fullscreen; H / Esc returns to the menu.
  • Vanilla / Max: Space uses one bomb to remove half the zombies; N uses two bombs to remove all of them.
  • Vanilla / Aleksa: M toggles sound. Max 0.1 / 0.2: U mutes, T toggles music, and M changes squad size instead of sound.
  • Max 0.2: turn off the horror-effects setting if you do not want those effects.

Castle Duel

Two players take turns on one device. Use ↑ / ↓ to adjust the angle, ← / → to adjust power, and Space to fire. Touch users can use the angle, power, and “FIRE” buttons. Watch the wind and trajectory preview.

The match is best of three rounds. After a round, use the continue button or Space. Use the “Games” button to leave. Help opens in a separate tab, but a shot already in flight may finish while you read.

Troubleshooting

Black screen or game will not load

Check your connection and any visible error. Minecraft needs WebGL2 and an HTTP/HTTPS address; local play requires the repository server. Avoid reloading with unsaved edits. Minecraft 0.3 targets Chrome on macOS; mobile and other-browser support varies by game.

Mouse, keyboard, or audio problems

Click the playfield and check pause and sound controls. In Minecraft, Esc releases the mouse; no-capture mode is also available. Audio may require a first click. Check browser-tab and system volume too.

Where are my saves?

Use the same site address, browser, and profile. Localhost saves do not automatically appear online. Do not clear site data as a first troubleshooting step. Minecraft 0.3 can import an exported backup; deleted browser data may be unrecoverable without one.

Report a problem

Include the game and version, browser, device, reproduction steps, and visible error. Do not post world files or full logs containing private information. GitHub sign-in is required to file an issue.
