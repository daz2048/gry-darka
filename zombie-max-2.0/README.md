## Scenery

- **Easy**: overgrown cemetery with an ivy-covered mausoleum, barred doorway, mossy stone paths, engraved gravestones, flowers, grass, drifting mist, and fireflies.
- **Normal**: abandoned city with a boarded pharmacy, striped awning, fire escape, sagging cables, a wrecked car, road markings, a drain cover, scattered paper, rain, and puddle ripples.
- **Hard**: industrial ruins with a storage tank, pipework and valves, a trussed crane, hanging hook, banded barrels, striped barricades, smoking chimneys, and rain.
- **Super Hard Demon**: volcanic fortress with a chained rune gateway, fractured spires, obsidian shards, glowing lava fissures, a ground sigil, and rising embers.
- Every mode includes ground cracks, rubble, supply crates, lanterns and light cones, plus an armored base with panel fasteners, vents, cable runs, and a status console.
- Base warning lights turn red at low health, and damage cracks increase as health falls; repairs clear the corresponding wear.
- Static scenery is cached per difficulty. New weather and lighting effects freeze when paused and respect reduced-motion preferences. Scenery is decorative and does not change movement, hits, or difficulty.

Easy, Normal, and Hard also feature eclipsed moonlight, overhead tendrils, long shadows, and slow nightmare mist. Easy keeps its twisted cemetery trees, Normal keeps the original city buildings without crystal-like towers, and Hard has glowing furnaces.

Super Hard Demon has a painted infernal fortress: layered ash clouds, distant ridges, etched lightning, and a horned stone idol with watching eyes, carved runes, eroded features, and a shadowed entrance. Torn woven banners, candle wax, chipped paving, stone pitting, and fine brush textures give the scene a weathered mural finish. This artwork is cached and stays behind enemies and pickups. Other modes and gameplay difficulty are unchanged.

Its dimensional layer adds a vast watching aperture in the sky, suspended broken arches, stairs leading nowhere, fractured sky seams, and a starfield beneath a torn courtyard. A faint drifting veil and upward-falling ash add slow movement without strobing or camera shake. These additional atmospheric effects respect Groza, reduced motion, pauses, game-over states, and hidden tabs; the painted scenery remains static.

The nightmare scenes add cracked, candlelit memorials and hanging lichen in Easy; torn missing-person notices, boarded doors, and chalk tallies in Normal; and riveted pressure doors, gauges, and suspended chains in Hard. These weathered details are cached with the scenery. Pale watching faces breathe slowly and follow the squad with their eyes when Groza and motion effects are enabled; their animation stops during pauses and in hidden tabs.

### Additional Detail

- **Easy** adds a cemetery bell tower, wrought-iron gate, moss patches, mushrooms, and patched scarves on enemies.
- **Normal** adds a broken-glass bus shelter, metro stairwell, traffic lights, and collared enemy outfits with loose ties.
- **Hard** adds a grated ventilation turbine, service stairs, roller conveyor, reflective workwear, and hard hats.
- **Super Hard Demon** adds shattered arches, hanging chains, braziers, basalt paving, and plated enemy armor.
- All modes include ground tracks, spent casings, enemy contact shadows, mini-boss equipment emblems, and radio packs on squad specialists.
- Mobs have more human proportions, jointed knees and elbows, hands, worn boots, shaped jaws, recessed eyes, and muted directional skin lighting. Cloth folds and curved, shaded armor replace the flat oval bodies and boxy shoulder plates. Boss markings, difficulty outfits, and golden-wave accents remain recognizable. Added gait motion stops during pauses, game-over states, and hidden tabs; reduced motion uses a still pose. Health, speed, hitboxes, and damage are unchanged.
- Loot diamonds have highlighted edges and symbols for their effect families, covering all 18 drop types. These details do not change statistics, collisions, or controls.

## Horror Atmosphere

- All four modes feature five irregular encounters: watching eyes, looming silhouettes, a pale apparition, a figure peeking around a pillar, and a distant procession of long-limbed shadows.
- Encounters fade in after an 8-14 second warmup, never repeat consecutively, and leave randomized quiet periods between them. Harder modes shorten those quiet periods; peeking figures and processions linger longer before fading.
- Quiet positional radio static, double heartbeats, filtered breathing, and distant footsteps build tension without increasing the jump-scare volume. They respect mute and audio autoplay restrictions.
- The **Groza** switch beside the music control disables encounters, jump scares, and the animated film overlay without restarting the game.
- Jump scares have an eight-second cooldown. Added encounters freeze when paused or the tab is hidden; reduced-motion mode disables the apparitions and jump scares.
- Apparitions render behind gameplay objects and do not change enemies, hit detection, or player controls.

## 🛒 Sklep

- Kategorie: **Guns** i **Skins**
- Kupujesz za licznik **Zabici**
- W **Guns** masz klasyczne upgrady oraz listę kupowanych broni
- W **Skins** kupujesz i zakładasz skiny ekipy
- Przycisk **Sklep** pojawia się też na ekranie przegranej

## ⌨️ Sterowanie

- **↑ / W** — ruch ekipy w górę
- **↓ / S** — ruch ekipy w dół
- **SPACEBAR** — bomba zwykła
- **N** — bomba atomowa
- **P** — pauza
- **F** — pełny ekran
- **R** — restart
- **U** — wyciszenie
- **ESC / H** — powrót do menu

## ⚡ Skróty specjalne Max 2.0

- **0** — ustaw ekipę na 100
- **2** — ustaw ekipę na 2000
- **9** — ustaw moc na 900
- **B** — ustaw moc na 100
- **O** — ustaw bomby na 200
- **1** — skok na falę 100
- **8** — skok na falę 1000
- **6** — infinite money (INF)

## 💡 Porady

- Rekrutuj strategicznie
- Zaoszczędzaj bomby na trudne momenty
